# Company FAQ

## General

Q: What are your support hours?
A: Our support team is available Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 WIB.

Q: How can I contact support?
A: You can contact us via email at support@company.com.

Q: How long does it take to receive a response?
A: Most requests receive a response within one business day.

---

## Billing

Q: How do I update my payment method?
A: Log in to your account, open Billing Settings, and click Update Payment Method.

Q: When will I be charged?
A: Subscription charges are processed automatically on your billing date every month.

Q: Can I download invoices?
A: Yes. Go to Billing > Invoices to download all available invoices.

---

## Refund Policy

Q: Can I request a refund?
A: Refund requests are accepted only within 7 days after the initial purchase.

Q: Can I get a refund after 30 days?
A: No. Refund requests submitted after 7 days are not eligible.

Q: Will unused subscription time be refunded?
A: No. Partial refunds are not provided for unused subscription periods.

---

## Technical Support

Q: I forgot my password.
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Q: The application is running slowly.
A: Please clear your browser cache and try again. If the issue continues, contact support.

Q: I cannot log in.
A: Verify your email and password. If the problem persists, reset your password or contact support.

---

## Security

Q: How do I change my password?
A: Go to Account Settings > Security > Change Password.

Q: Is my data encrypted?
A: Yes. All sensitive data is encrypted during transmission and storage.

Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication?
A: Open Security Settings and enable Two-Factor Authentication.

---

## Escalation

The following issues must be escalated to a human support agent immediately:

- Data loss
- Security breach
- Service outage
- Customer contacted support more than three times within the last seven days

The AI assistant must not provide troubleshooting steps before escalation for these cases.
